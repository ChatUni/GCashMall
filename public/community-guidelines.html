Ganime Content & Community Guidelines

Create freely. Share responsibly. Respect everyone.

Operated by Fresh Road Studio Inc.

Version 1.0 — Effective November 11, 2026

Welcome to the Ganime Community

Ganime is a place where anime fans discover stories and creators bring their imagination to life.

Whether you're watching your favorite series, creating original anime using AI, or sharing your work with others, we want Ganime to be creative, welcoming, and safe.

These Guidelines explain what content and behavior are allowed on Ganime, what is prohibited, and how we keep the community safe.

They apply to everyone using Ganime, including viewers, creators, and other community participants.

They cover anime episodes, AI-generated videos, images, prompts, scripts, titles, thumbnails, profiles, comments, and other content or interactions on the platform.

By using Ganime, you agree to follow these Guidelines and our Terms of Service.

1. Who Can Use Ganime

Ganime has different age requirements for viewers and creators.

Users aged 13–17 may access eligible viewer features, subject to age-appropriate safety restrictions.

Users under 18 cannot access creator features, even with parental or guardian consent.

Ganime may require age verification and restrict access to features or content based on age and applicable law.

2. What You Can Create

Ganime welcomes original stories and creative expression across many anime genres.

Examples of generally permitted content include:

  • Action and adventure: Superheroes, fantasy battles, martial arts, and exciting adventures.
  • Romance and relationships: Love stories, friendships, and emotional drama.
  • Comedy: Humor, parody, everyday situations, and entertaining characters.
  • Fantasy and science fiction: Magic, supernatural worlds, futuristic technology, and imaginative creatures.
  • Mystery and suspense: Detective stories, mysteries, and age-appropriate thrillers.
  • Horror: Supernatural stories, suspense, and non-graphic horror.
  • Slice of life: School life, family stories, friendship, personal growth, and everyday experiences.
  • Educational and inspirational: Stories that teach, motivate, or explore meaningful subjects.

Both traditional and AI-assisted creative work are welcome.

Our guiding principle: Be imaginative, tell original stories, and respect the rights and safety of others.

3. Sexual Content & Nudity

Ganime is not an adult-content platform.

We do not allow pornography or content primarily intended to cause sexual arousal.

Prohibited content includes:

  • Explicit sexual acts or graphic sexual activity.
  • Pornographic animation, hentai, or sexually explicit AI-generated material.
  • Sexual exploitation, coercion, or nonconsensual sexual content.
  • Sexualized depictions of minors or characters presented as minors.
  • Sexual content involving a person who cannot consent.
  • Nonconsensual intimate images or sexual deepfakes.
  • Content promoting sexual exploitation or trafficking.

These restrictions apply whether the content is animated, AI-generated, realistic, or fictional.

Generally allowed, when presented appropriately:

  • Romantic stories and relationships.
  • Kissing, hugging, and non-explicit affection.
  • Non-graphic romantic themes.
  • Nonsexual nudity in a legitimate artistic, medical, or educational context, subject to review and age restrictions.

Ganime may reject or restrict sexualized imagery, suggestive thumbnails, or provocative content even when it does not depict explicit sexual activity.

Age labels do not make otherwise prohibited sexual content acceptable.

4. Violence, Horror & Disturbing Content

Anime often includes action, fantasy combat, and dramatic conflict.

Ganime allows fictional action and non-graphic violence, but places limits on extreme or harmful material.

Generally allowed:

  • Fantasy battles and martial arts.
  • Superhero or adventure combat.
  • Non-graphic injuries.
  • Fictional conflict and suspense.
  • Mild or moderate horror without extreme graphic detail.

Restricted or prohibited:

  • Extremely graphic gore, mutilation, or torture.
  • Realistic depictions of severe cruelty intended primarily to shock or disturb.
  • Content celebrating or encouraging real-world violence.
  • Threats or calls for violence against real people or groups.
  • Content encouraging terrorism or violent extremism.
  • Detailed instructions intended to facilitate violent wrongdoing.

Ganime may apply additional review or age restrictions to intense but otherwise permissible fictional content.

Content that exceeds Ganime's safety standards may be removed regardless of its fictional or artistic context.

5. Child Safety

Protecting minors is a priority.

Ganime strictly prohibits:

  • Child sexual abuse material, including AI-generated or animated material.
  • Sexual exploitation or sexualized depictions of minors.
  • Grooming or attempts to establish exploitative relationships with minors.
  • Soliciting inappropriate images or personal information from minors.
  • Content encouraging abuse, exploitation, or trafficking of children.
  • Predatory behavior directed toward minors.

These rules apply to realistic, fictional, illustrated, and AI-generated content.

Ganime may immediately remove prohibited material, suspend or terminate accounts, and report suspected exploitation to appropriate authorities or child-protection organizations when legally required or otherwise appropriate.

We may preserve relevant records where necessary to comply with legal obligations and support investigations.

6. Copyright, Fan Anime & Intellectual Property

Ganime encourages original anime stories and characters.

Creators must have the rights and permissions needed to publish and monetize their content.

You may not knowingly infringe another person's:

  • Copyrighted anime, videos, or artwork.
  • Characters, stories, scripts, or protected designs.
  • Music, songs, sound recordings, or voice performances.
  • Trademarks, logos, or other intellectual-property rights.

Fan-created anime

Fan art, fan fiction, parody, and stories inspired by existing works can be part of creative culture. However, publishing or monetizing material based on existing franchises may require authorization from the relevant rights holders.

Giving credit, making changes, or generating material with AI does not automatically make an otherwise infringing use lawful.

Certain uses may be permitted by law, including applicable copyright exceptions such as fair use. These questions depend on the specific circumstances.

Ganime may remove content that violates intellectual-property rights or its policies.

For formal copyright complaints and counter-notifications, please see our Copyright & DMCA Policy.

7. AI-Generated Content & Responsible Creation

Ganime provides AI tools, including Quick Create, to help eligible adult creators turn ideas into anime.

AI-generated content must follow the same rules as other content published on Ganime.

Creators are responsible for:

  • Reviewing outputs before publishing.
  • Ensuring they have the rights to reference materials.
  • Avoiding unauthorized use of copyrighted content.
  • Avoiding misleading or harmful impersonations.
  • Following applicable AI-provider terms.
  • Complying with these Guidelines and applicable law.

Prohibited AI uses include:

  • Generating sexual exploitation or other prohibited sexual content.
  • Creating deceptive or harmful impersonations.
  • Producing unauthorized intimate deepfakes.
  • Cloning a real person's voice or likeness without required authorization.
  • Generating content intended to facilitate fraud, threats, harassment, or other unlawful activity.
  • Attempting to bypass Ganime's AI safety systems.

AI-generated content may resemble existing works and may not qualify for copyright protection in every jurisdiction.

Ganime does not guarantee the originality or legal status of AI-generated outputs.

Where required by law or Ganime's policies, creators must disclose that content is AI-generated or materially AI-altered.

8. Real People, Voices & Impersonation

Creators must respect the privacy, identity, and likeness of real people.

You may not use another person's image, voice, identity, or personal information in ways that violate their rights.

Prohibited conduct includes:

  • Creating deceptive impersonations intended to mislead or defraud.
  • Producing unauthorized sexual deepfakes.
  • Using someone's likeness to falsely imply endorsement.
  • Impersonating public officials or other people to facilitate scams or harmful deception.
  • Sharing private personal information without authorization.

Fictional portrayals, commentary, satire, and parody may be permitted when lawful and not otherwise prohibited by these Guidelines.

Where appropriate, Ganime may require labeling or other measures to reduce the risk of confusion about AI-generated portrayals.

9. Harassment, Hate Speech & Bullying

Ganime should be a welcoming community for viewers and creators.

We do not permit targeted harassment, bullying, stalking, or threats.

Prohibited behavior includes:

  • Threatening or repeatedly harassing another user.
  • Encouraging others to attack or intimidate someone.
  • Publishing private information to facilitate harassment.
  • Hate speech that attacks or dehumanizes people based on protected characteristics.
  • Encouraging discrimination or violence against protected groups.
  • Abusive behavior intended to silence, intimidate, or humiliate other users.

Disagreement, criticism, debate, and fictional conflict are not automatically violations.

Ganime considers context, including whether material is fictional, educational, documentary, critical, or intended to promote harmful behavior.

We may remove content or restrict accounts that repeatedly engage in abusive conduct.

10. Dangerous, Illegal & Harmful Activity

Ganime does not permit content or activity that facilitates serious unlawful harm.

Examples include:

  • Instructions intended to facilitate violent crimes.
  • Fraud, identity theft, phishing, or financial scams.
  • Promotion or coordination of terrorism or violent extremism.
  • Sale or distribution of illegal goods or services.
  • Content encouraging dangerous challenges likely to result in serious injury.
  • Content encouraging self-harm or suicide.

Fictional stories, educational material, news reporting, recovery discussions, and other legitimate expression may be permitted when they do not promote or facilitate harmful activity.

Ganime may restrict or remove content when necessary to protect users or comply with applicable law.

11. Community Behavior

Ganime viewers may be able to comment, like, follow, review, share, or otherwise interact with creators and their work.

Please treat others respectfully.

We encourage you to:

  • Support creators whose work you enjoy.
  • Offer constructive feedback.
  • Respect different creative styles and opinions.
  • Report harmful or inappropriate material.
  • Give credit where appropriate.
  • Respect other users' privacy and boundaries.

Do not:

  • Spam comments or promotional messages.
  • Post scams, malicious links, or phishing attempts.
  • Use bots or fake accounts to manipulate engagement.
  • Purchase or sell fake followers, likes, comments, or views.
  • Impersonate other creators or users.
  • Repeatedly contact someone after being blocked.
  • Abuse reporting or moderation systems.

Ganime may limit certain community features to protect users, particularly younger viewers.

12. Spam, Fake Engagement & Monetization Abuse

Creators must earn through legitimate viewer interest and authorized purchases.

Ganime prohibits activity that artificially increases views, engagement, purchases, or Creator Earnings.

Examples include:

  • Buying or generating fake views, likes, or followers.
  • Creating multiple accounts to abuse rewards or promotions.
  • Using automated systems to simulate engagement.
  • Coordinating fake purchases to generate Creator Earnings.
  • Manipulating refunds, chargebacks, or Credit transactions.
  • Misleading viewers about the content they will receive.

Creators must not use deceptive titles, descriptions, previews, or thumbnails to misrepresent paid content.

Ganime may remove fraudulent activity, restrict monetization, adjust invalid earnings, or terminate accounts.

Creator Earnings and payout adjustments are governed by the Ganime Creator Agreement.

13. Content Titles, Thumbnails & Descriptions

Titles, thumbnails, and descriptions help viewers decide what to watch.

They should accurately represent the content.

Creators should:

  • Use titles that reasonably describe the episode or series.
  • Choose thumbnails that reflect the actual content.
  • Apply available content labels accurately.
  • Avoid misleading claims about copyright ownership or endorsements.
  • Avoid inappropriate or prohibited imagery.

Ganime may edit or remove misleading metadata, request changes, restrict distribution, or reject publication.

A series must not use a harmless-looking thumbnail or title to conceal content that violates these Guidelines.

14. Content Ratings & Age-Appropriate Viewing

Ganime is designed for viewers aged 13 and older.

Some anime themes may be suitable for younger teens, while others may be more intense.

Ganime may classify content into categories such as:

ClassificationDescription
GeneralBroadly suitable content with minimal potentially sensitive material
TeenSuitable for eligible teen viewers; may include moderate action, romance, language, or suspense
Mature 18+More intense permitted themes that are not appropriate for minors and are available only where Ganime supports restricted adult viewing

These are proposed Ganime classifications, not official Apple or Google age ratings.

The Mature 18+ classification is not permission to publish pornography, extreme graphic violence, or otherwise prohibited content.

Ganime may choose not to distribute certain mature content through its mobile applications.

Creators must provide accurate information about their content when requested.

Ganime may change ratings, restrict access, or remove content if the submitted classification is inaccurate or the content violates its rules.

Until age-gating and mature-content controls are fully implemented, Ganime may decline content requiring an 18+ restriction.

15. Content Review & Moderation

Ganime may review content before or after publication.

We may use automated tools and human reviewers to identify possible policy violations.

Review may include:

  • Video and image analysis.
  • Text, script, and title screening.
  • Audio and speech review.
  • Copyright-related checks.
  • User reports.
  • AI safety and abuse detection.

Moderation actions may include:

  • Approving content for publication.
  • Requesting edits before publication.
  • Assigning or changing a content rating.
  • Restricting recommendations or age-based access.
  • Removing episodes, series, comments, or other material.
  • Suspending creator privileges or monetization.
  • Temporarily or permanently suspending accounts.

Verification or previous approval does not exempt a creator from these Guidelines.

Ganime may take immediate action against serious violations.

16. Reporting Content & Blocking Users

If you see content or behavior that violates these Guidelines, please report it.

Where supported, you can use the reporting option associated with a video, series, comment, or user profile.

Reports may cover:

  • Sexual or exploitative material.
  • Violence or threats.
  • Harassment or bullying.
  • Hate speech.
  • Copyright concerns.
  • Impersonation or privacy violations.
  • Spam, scams, or fraud.
  • Other policy violations.

You may also contact support@ganime.io.

Users may block abusive accounts through available in-app controls. Blocking restricts interactions according to the functionality provided by Ganime.

Ganime reviews reports according to severity, available information, and legal requirements.

We may prioritize urgent reports involving child safety, credible threats, or serious abuse.

Submitting a report does not guarantee removal. Ganime will assess the circumstances and applicable policies.

Formal copyright takedown requests must follow our Copyright & DMCA Policy.

17. Warnings, Restrictions & Account Termination

Not all violations are equally serious.

Ganime may consider the severity, frequency, and circumstances of violations, as well as the risk of harm to others.

Possible enforcement actions include:

  1. Warning: Notification and request to correct a problem.
  2. Content restriction: Removal, limited visibility, or required editing.
  3. Feature restriction: Temporary limits on commenting, publishing, or other features.
  4. Monetization suspension: Temporary or permanent restrictions on creator earnings.
  5. Account termination: Permanent removal for serious or repeated violations.

Serious violations, including child exploitation, credible violent threats, severe abuse, or deliberate infringement, may result in immediate termination without prior warning.

Where appropriate, Ganime may provide a way to appeal moderation decisions.

Creators with legitimate outstanding earnings remain subject to the payment and adjustment provisions of the Creator Agreement.

18. Appeals & Reconsideration

If you believe a moderation decision was made in error, you may request a review through an available appeal feature or by contacting Ganime Support.

Please include:

  • Your account or creator name.
  • The affected episode, series, comment, or other content.
  • The reason you believe the decision was incorrect.
  • Any relevant explanation or supporting information.

Ganime may review the decision and uphold, modify, or reverse it.

We cannot guarantee that every appeal will result in restoration.

Copyright takedown disputes subject to the DMCA have a separate formal counter-notification process described in the Copyright & DMCA Policy.

19. Updates to These Guidelines

Ganime may update these Guidelines to respond to new features, emerging AI technologies, community needs, platform requirements, or changes in applicable law.

The latest version will be posted on the Ganime website.

For material changes, Ganime may provide additional notice through the Service or email where appropriate or legally required.

Continued use of Ganime after updated Guidelines take effect constitutes acceptance to the extent permitted by applicable law.
