Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ganime (“Ganime”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), operated by FreshRoad Inc., collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Ganime mobile app and website at ganime.io (the “Service”). Ganime lets you watch AI-generated anime series and create your own animated episodes.

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account information — when you sign up with email/password or a third-party login (e.g. Google), we receive your email address and may receive your name and profile picture. A default display name and avatar are assigned if none is provided.
  • Content you create or upload — the story ideas and prompts you enter, and any videos, series, episodes, titles, descriptions, and images you generate, upload, or publish through the Service.
  • Payment and wallet activity — records of top-ups, withdrawals, purchases, and your in-app GUSD balance. Card payments are processed by our payment provider; in-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Communications — messages, feedback, or support requests you send us.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and analytics data — how you interact with the Service (pages/screens viewed, features used, sessions), collected via Google Analytics on our website.
  • Device and log data — device type, operating system, app version, general location inferred from IP address, and diagnostic/log information.
  • Local storage — we store a login token and preferences on your device to keep you signed in and remember your settings.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service, including your account and wallet.
  • Generate anime content from your prompts and produce, host, and stream your episodes.
  • Process payments, top-ups, withdrawals, and unlock purchases.
  • Moderate uploaded and generated content for safety and to enforce our terms.
  • Communicate with you about your account, transactions, and support requests.
  • Analyze and improve the Service, and develop new features.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. AI Processing of Your Content

To generate and moderate content, the prompts you enter and the media you upload or generate are processed by third-party artificial-intelligence providers on our behalf (for example, to write stories, design characters, synthesize video and audio, transcribe speech, and screen content for safety). This processing is necessary to provide the core features of the Service.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information as described below.

7. Your Rights and Choices

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and hashed passwords. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

10. International Users

We operate the Service globally and may process and store information in countries other than where you live, which may have different data-protection laws. By using the Service, you consent to such transfers.
