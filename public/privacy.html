Privacy Policy

Fresh Road Studio Inc.

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Ganime (“Ganime,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is operated by Fresh Road Studio Inc. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you use the Ganime mobile application, the website at ganime.io, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

Ganime allows users to discover and watch AI-generated anime series and to create, upload, publish, and monetize animated stories and episodes.

This Privacy Policy is a notice of our privacy practices. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular activity, we will request that consent separately.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information processed through the Service. It does not apply to third-party websites, applications, or services that are governed by their own privacy policies.

Fresh Road Studio Inc. is the controller of personal information described in this Privacy Policy, except where another entity is identified as the controller at the point of collection.

2. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use the Service and which features are available in your region.

A. Information you provide

  • Account and profile information. Your email address, password or authentication credentials, display name, username, profile picture, age or date-of-birth information if requested, and information received through a third-party sign-in provider such as Google or Apple. If you do not provide a display name or avatar, the Service may assign default values.
  • Prompts and creator content. Story ideas, prompts, scripts, character descriptions, reference materials, images, audio, video, titles, descriptions, series, episodes, and other content that you create, upload, generate, save, or publish.
  • Community activity. Comments, likes, follows, favorites, reports, messages, and other interactions with creators, viewers, and content, if these features are available.
  • Payment and transaction information. Purchase history, subscriptions, in-app purchases, top-ups, withdrawals, creator earnings, revenue shares, payout status, transaction identifiers, and GUSD balance and activity. Payment providers generally collect payment-card or bank details directly, and we do not receive or store complete payment-card numbers.
  • Identity, wallet, and payout information. If needed to provide GUSD, wallet, withdrawal, creator-payout, tax, fraud-prevention, sanctions-screening, or legally required compliance features, we or our providers may collect legal name, address, date of birth, government-issued identification, tax information, bank or payout account details, wallet addresses, and verification results.
  • Communications. Information contained in support requests, feedback, surveys, complaints, appeals, and other communications with us.

B. Information collected automatically

  • Usage information. Pages and screens viewed, searches, watch history, content interactions, features used, session activity, referral information, and the dates and times of access.
  • Device and log information. IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, app version, language, device and application identifiers, crash data, diagnostic information, and security logs.
  • Approximate location. Country, state, region, or city inferred from an IP address. We do not collect precise device location unless we provide a feature that requires it and give you appropriate notice and choice.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. Cookies, local storage, software development kits, pixels, and similar technologies used to keep you signed in, remember preferences, measure use, maintain security, and improve performance.
  • Push-notification information. A device push token and related preferences if you enable notifications.

C. Information from other sources

We may receive information from authentication providers, app stores, payment and payout providers, wallet and identity-verification providers, analytics and fraud-prevention services, other users who interact with or report content, and public sources where permitted by law.

D. Sensitive information and information about other people

The Service is not designed for users to submit highly sensitive personal information in prompts or content unless it is specifically requested for a supported feature. Please do not include government identification numbers, financial credentials, health information, or other confidential personal information in a prompt or upload unless necessary for a feature that clearly requests it.

If you upload an image, voice recording, video, or other information about another person, you are responsible for having the rights and permissions needed to provide that material to the Service. Ganime does not use facial or voice information to uniquely identify or authenticate a person unless we provide separate notice and obtain any consent required by law.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • Create, authenticate, maintain, and secure accounts.
  • Provide, operate, personalize, and maintain the Service.
  • Process prompts and generate stories, characters, images, audio, video, and animated episodes.
  • Save, host, encode, deliver, recommend, and stream content.
  • Process subscriptions, purchases, top-ups, GUSD transactions, withdrawals, creator earnings, refunds, and payouts.
  • Verify identity, prevent fraud, perform sanctions or compliance screening, and satisfy financial or tax requirements when applicable.
  • Moderate uploaded and generated content; investigate reports; enforce our Terms of Service and community rules; and provide appeal mechanisms.
  • Provide customer support and respond to questions, requests, and complaints.
  • Send transactional communications such as receipts, security alerts, password resets, service notices, and payout updates.
  • Send product news or promotional communications where permitted. You may unsubscribe from promotional email, but you may still receive necessary account and transactional messages.
  • Measure usage, troubleshoot problems, improve performance, understand audience interests, and develop new features.
  • Protect users, Ganime, Fresh Road Studio Inc., our providers, and the public from fraud, abuse, security threats, illegal activity, and harm.
  • Comply with law, regulatory requirements, legal process, and enforceable governmental requests; establish or defend legal claims; and enforce our agreements.

We may create aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be linked to you and use it for analytics, research, business planning, and service improvement. We will not attempt to re-identify information that applicable law requires us to maintain in de-identified form.

4. AI Processing of Content

Ganime uses artificial-intelligence technologies to provide its core features. Prompts, instructions, uploaded media, generated content, and associated technical information may be sent to AI service providers to perform functions such as:

  • Story and dialogue generation.
  • Character and image generation.
  • Video and animation generation.
  • Voice, music, sound, and speech processing.
  • Speech transcription and translation.
  • Content classification, safety screening, and moderation.

AI providers may temporarily retain content and related technical data to provide their services, maintain security, detect abuse, and comply with law. Retention varies by provider, feature, and technical configuration.

Unless we provide separate notice and obtain your permission where required, Ganime does not use your private prompts or private uploads to train general-purpose AI models, and we do not authorize our AI service providers to use that private content to train general-purpose models for their own benefit. Published content may be used to operate, promote, moderate, and improve the Ganime Service as described in our Terms of Service, but it will not be used for model training beyond those terms without additional notice or permission where required.

AI systems and automated moderation tools may make mistakes. Where the Service provides a content-moderation appeal process, you may request review of an affected decision.

5. Public Content and Community Features

Your drafts are not public unless you choose to publish or share them through a public or shared feature. When you publish content, your display name, profile picture, series, episodes, descriptions, and related community activity may be visible to other users and may be shared outside the Service.

Public content may be indexed by search engines or copied, downloaded, recorded, or reshared by other people. If you later delete or unpublish content, we will take reasonable steps to remove it from active Ganime surfaces, but copies controlled by other people, search engines, caches, or third-party services may remain.

6. Payments, GUSD, Wallets, and Creator Earnings

Payments may be processed by Stripe, Apple, Google, or other authorized providers. Those providers process payment information under their own privacy notices. Ganime receives information necessary to confirm and administer a transaction, such as a transaction identifier, purchase status, amount, currency, subscription status, or limited billing information.

If Ganime offers GUSD, wallet, withdrawal, or creator-payout functionality, personal information may be shared with the financial-infrastructure, wallet, payment, identity-verification, banking, tax, fraud-prevention, sanctions-screening, or payout providers needed to complete the requested service and comply with law.

If a GUSD or wallet transaction is recorded on a public blockchain, the wallet address, transaction identifier, amount, time, and related blockchain information may be publicly visible and permanently recorded. Ganime generally cannot edit or delete information recorded on a public blockchain.

7. Cookies, Analytics, and Similar Technologies

We use necessary cookies and local storage to authenticate users, keep accounts secure, maintain sessions, remember settings, and provide requested features. On our website, Google Analytics may use cookies and process usage, device, browser, online identifier, and IP-derived approximate location information to measure traffic and improve the Service.

We configure Google Analytics for analytics and Service improvement, not targeted advertising. Where required, we obtain consent before using non-essential analytics. You may manage available choices through our cookie controls or browser settings. Disabling necessary storage may prevent portions of the Service from functioning correctly.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. These safeguards include encrypted network connections, access controls, monitoring, and secure password handling. Where Ganime stores passwords, they are stored using one-way cryptographic hashing rather than in readable form.

No transmission or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and notifying us if you suspect unauthorized access.
