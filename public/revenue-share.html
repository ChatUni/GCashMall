2. Who Can Become a Creator

Users under 18 are not permitted to create, upload, publish, or monetize content through Ganime's creator features, even with parental or guardian consent.

By becoming a Creator, you confirm that:

  • You are at least 18 years old.
  • You have the legal capacity to enter into this Agreement.
  • You provide accurate account and payment information.
  • You comply with applicable laws and Ganime's policies.

Ganime may require identity and age verification before allowing publication, monetization, or withdrawals.

If Ganime determines that a Creator is under 18, we may disable creator access, remove or unpublish content, and restrict monetization. Any valid outstanding earnings will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

3. How Viewers Pay

Viewers use Ganime Credits to unlock episodes and series.

Individual episodes

  • The first five episodes of every series are free to watch.
  • Each additional episode costs 25 Credits.
  • Ganime sets these episode prices for all creators.

Whole-series purchases

You may choose a whole-series price of 300, 600, or 900 Credits. If you do not choose, the default is 600 Credits.

A whole-series purchase is offered only when it costs less than purchasing the viewer's remaining locked episodes individually.

Credits the viewer has already spent unlocking individual episodes in that series are deducted from the whole-series price, up to the amount of that price.

Important: A whole-series purchase includes all currently available episodes and future episodes added to the same series, without additional episode charges.

Creators should consider this when selecting a whole-series price.

Ganime may introduce additional purchase options, including season passes, subscriptions, promotional bundles, or other monetization features. Any additional monetization terms will be disclosed before they apply.

4. Your Revenue Share

You earn 70% of eligible Credits viewers spend unlocking your episodes or series.

Ganime keeps the remaining 30%.

Your share is calculated before applicable app store commissions and payment-processing fees. Ganime covers those fees.

Examples

Viewer purchaseYour earnings
1 episode — 25 Credits17.5 Credits ($0.175)
Whole series — 300 Credits210 Credits ($2.10)
Whole series — 600 Credits420 Credits ($4.20)
Whole series — 900 Credits630 Credits ($6.30)

If a whole-series purchase is discounted because the viewer already bought individual episodes, your 70% share applies to the additional eligible Credits actually paid.

Free episodes do not generate earnings.

Paid and Promotional Credits

  • Paid Credits: Credits purchased by viewers are eligible for normal creator earnings.
  • Promotional Credits: Free Credits, welcome bonuses, rewards, and complimentary Credits issued by Ganime do not generate Creator Earnings unless Ganime expressly states otherwise for a particular promotion.
  • Mixed payments: When a purchase uses both Paid Credits and Promotional Credits, Creator Earnings are calculated only on the eligible Paid Credit portion, unless different promotional terms are disclosed.

Ganime may offer special promotional programs that compensate creators differently. Any applicable creator compensation will be disclosed in the promotion's terms.

Creator Earnings are calculated using 100 Creator Earnings Credits = US$1.00, even if viewers purchase Credits through different pricing packages or payment channels.

5. When You Earn

Your share is credited to your Ganime wallet when an eligible viewer purchase is completed.

You can view your earnings in:

Account → Wallet

Each transaction may show the relevant series, purchase, eligible Credits, creator share, and transaction status.

Earnings may appear as:

  • Pending: Within the 30-day holding period.
  • Available: Eligible for withdrawal.
  • Adjusted: Changed because of a refund, chargeback, reversal, or other permitted adjustment.

Earnings are calculated using the revenue-share rate in effect when the purchase occurs.

Changes to future rates do not retroactively reduce earnings already credited from valid transactions, except for adjustments permitted under this Agreement.

6. Getting Paid

Creator Earnings become eligible for withdrawal after a 30-day holding period, provided the related transaction remains valid.

The holding period allows time for refunds, chargebacks, fraud prevention, and payment verification.

To request a withdrawal:

Account → Wallet → Withdraw

The minimum withdrawal amount is US$10, equivalent to 1,000 Creator Earnings Credits.

Eligible earnings are converted at:

100 Creator Earnings Credits = US$1.00

Withdrawal requests below US$10 are generally not processed, except where applicable law requires otherwise or Ganime permits a final account-closure payout.

Withdrawals are processed through Ganime's supported payout providers, subject to their terms and applicable requirements.

Available payout methods, processing times, and any withdrawal fees will be displayed before you confirm a withdrawal.

Ganime may require identity verification, age verification, tax information, payment-account verification, or other legally required checks.

You are responsible for applicable taxes on your Creator Earnings. Ganime may make legally required tax withholdings or issue tax reporting documents.

Withdrawals may be unavailable or restricted in certain countries or regions.

Purchased viewer Credits, welcome bonuses, and Promotional Credits are not withdrawable as Creator Earnings merely because they are held in your wallet.

Ganime may delay a withdrawal when reasonably necessary to investigate suspected fraud, comply with law, resolve a payment dispute, or complete required verification.

7. Refunds, Fraud & Earnings Adjustments

If a viewer's purchase is refunded, charged back, reversed, or otherwise invalidated, Ganime may deduct the corresponding Creator Earnings from your wallet.

If the earnings have already been withdrawn, Ganime may offset the adjustment against future earnings or seek repayment where legally permitted.

You may not artificially generate earnings through fraudulent or manipulative activity.

Examples include:

  • Purchasing your own content using fake or controlled accounts.
  • Creating multiple accounts to claim Promotional Credits.
  • Using bots or automated purchasing schemes.
  • Coordinating fraudulent purchases to generate Creator Earnings.
  • Manipulating refund systems, payment processes, or viewership activity.

Ganime may investigate suspicious activity, withhold related earnings, remove content, suspend monetization, or terminate accounts.

We will not arbitrarily cancel legitimate Creator Earnings. Any withholding or adjustment must be connected to a reasonable contractual, legal, fraud-prevention, or payment-related basis.

Where appropriate, creators may contact Ganime Support to request a review of an earnings adjustment.
