Creator Revenue Share Agreement

Ganime, by Fresh Road Studio Inc. · Effective November 9, 2026

This agreement is between Fresh Road Studio Inc. ("Ganime", "we", "us") and you, the creator who publishes a series on Ganime ("you"). It explains how you earn from your series and how you get paid. It applies to every series you publish, whether you uploaded it or made it with Quick Create. By uploading or publishing a series, you accept it, along with our Privacy Policy.

2. How viewers pay

  • Viewers unlock episodes with Ganime credits. They buy credits in the app or on the website.
  • The first 5 episodes of every series are free to watch. Each episode after that costs 25 credits. Ganime sets these for all series.
  • You choose a whole-series price of 300, 600 or 900 credits (600 if you don't choose). A viewer is offered it only when it costs less than buying the remaining episodes one by one. Whatever they already spent on single episodes of the series is taken off. A whole-series unlock includes episodes you add later.

3. Your share

  • You earn 70% of the credits a viewer pays to unlock an episode or a whole series of yours.
  • Your share is worked out on the credit price, before app store and payment fees. Ganime pays those fees.
  • Examples at the current rates: a single episode earns you 17.5 credits (US$0.175). A whole series bought at 600 credits earns you 420 credits (US$4.20). A whole series sold at a discount, because the viewer already bought some episodes, earns your share of the discounted price.
  • Free episodes earn nothing.

4. When you are credited

  • Your share is added to your Ganime wallet the moment a viewer makes the purchase. It appears as an earning transaction in Account → Wallet.
  • Each earning uses the rate in effect when the purchase was made. If the rates change later, earnings already credited do not change.

5. Getting paid

  • Earnings can be withdrawn 30 days after they are credited. This hold covers refunds and fraud checks.
  • You withdraw from Account → Wallet → Withdraw. Withdrawals are paid in US dollars through our payment partner GUSD, at 100 credits = US$1.00.
  • The welcome bonus credits given to new accounts can't be withdrawn.
  • You are responsible for any taxes on your earnings. We may ask for the tax information the law requires before paying you.

6. Refunds, fraud and rule breaking

  • If a purchase of your content is refunded, charged back or reversed, we may take the matching earning back out of your wallet.
  • We may withhold or cancel earnings that come from fraud or manipulation. Examples include buying your own episodes through other accounts, using fake accounts, or paying people to buy.
  • We may withhold earnings from a series that breaks our content rules, and may remove that series.

7. Your content

  • You keep the rights you have in your series. You confirm you have the rights to everything you upload, including video, audio, music, images and text.
  • You give Ganime a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to host, stream, sell, translate and subtitle your series. The license also covers making thumbnails and previews, and promoting your series on Ganime and elsewhere.
  • Uploads are reviewed before they go public, unless your account has been verified. We may decline or remove content that breaks our rules.
  • A series that viewers have bought stays available to those viewers, even if you later unpublish it or delete your account. They paid for it.

8. Ending the agreement

  • You can stop at any time by unpublishing your series or deleting your account.
  • Withdraw your balance before you delete your account. Credits left in a deleted account are forfeited and can't be refunded.
  • We may end this agreement, or suspend payouts, if you break it or our content rules.

9. Changes

We may change the rates or this agreement. We will post the new version on this page. If we lower your share, we will tell you in the app or by email at least 30 days before the change takes effect. Changes never affect earnings already credited. If you keep publishing after a change takes effect, you accept the new version.

The rates on this page come from Ganime's live settings, so they are always the rates in effect today.
