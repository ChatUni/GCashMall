Ganime Copyright & DMCA Policy

Operated by Fresh Road Studio Inc.

Effective Date: November 11, 2026

At Ganime, we believe creators should be free to tell their stories while respecting the creative work of others.

Ganime allows creators to create, upload, publish, and monetize anime series, including content produced with AI tools such as Quick Create.

This Copyright & DMCA Policy explains how we protect intellectual-property rights, how copyright owners can report infringement, and what creators can do if their content is removed.

This Policy applies to the Ganime website, mobile applications, AI creation tools, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

Ganime is operated by Fresh Road Studio Inc. ("Ganime," "we," "us," or "our").

1. Respecting Copyright

Creators must own or have the necessary rights and permissions to publish and monetize their content.

Copyright-protected material may include:

  • Anime episodes, videos, and animation.
  • Characters, artwork, illustrations, and designs.
  • Stories, scripts, and dialogue.
  • Music, songs, sound effects, and audio recordings.
  • Photographs and other creative works.

Uploading content to Ganime does not give you ownership of someone else's work.

You may not publish or monetize copyrighted material without permission or another legally valid basis for using it.

Certain uses may qualify for exceptions such as fair use under applicable law. Fair use is evaluated case by case and is not guaranteed merely because content is transformed, edited, short, credited, or noncommercial.

2. Fan Anime & Existing Characters

Ganime welcomes original anime stories and characters.

However, creating content based on existing anime franchises, characters, or other copyrighted material can raise intellectual-property concerns.

Examples include using characters, animation, music, or artwork from an existing anime series without the rights holder's permission.

Important things to know:

  • Giving credit to the original creator does not replace permission.
  • Changing the appearance of a character does not automatically avoid infringement.
  • AI-generating an existing copyrighted character does not automatically make its use lawful.
  • Monetizing fan-created content does not establish a right to use someone else's intellectual property.
  • Some fan-created content may qualify for a legal exception, but that depends on the specific circumstances.

Ganime may remove or restrict fan-created content that infringes third-party rights or violates our policies.

3. AI-Generated Content

Ganime provides AI tools, including Quick Create, to help creators produce original anime.

AI-generated content is subject to the same copyright rules as uploaded content.

Creators are responsible for reviewing AI-generated outputs before publishing them.

You must not knowingly use AI tools to infringe copyrighted works, reproduce protected material without authorization, or misrepresent someone else's work as your own.

AI-generated content is not automatically copyright-free.

An AI-generated video may contain elements that resemble protected characters, artwork, music, or other creative material.

Ganime does not guarantee that AI-generated output is unique, qualifies for copyright protection, or is free of third-party claims.

Creators must also comply with applicable AI model provider terms and Ganime's Creator Agreement.

4. How to Report Copyright Infringement

If you believe content on Ganime infringes your copyright, you or your authorized representative may submit a copyright takedown notice.

To help us process your request, please provide the following information:

  1. Your signature. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on the owner's behalf.
  2. Your copyrighted work. Identification of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed. If multiple works are involved, provide a representative list where appropriate.
  3. The content on Ganime. A direct URL, series title, episode link, or other information reasonably sufficient for us to locate the allegedly infringing material.
  4. Your contact information. Your name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address, if available.
  5. Good-faith statement. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, the owner's agent, or the law.
  6. Accuracy statement. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the allegedly infringed exclusive right.

Send your notice to Ganime's designated copyright agent using the contact details in Section 11.

Please provide accurate and complete information. An incomplete notice may require additional clarification.

You do not need to create a Ganime account to submit a copyright complaint.

5. What Happens After a Copyright Complaint

When Ganime receives a copyright complaint, we may:

  • Review the notice for required information.
  • Request additional information if necessary.
  • Remove or disable access to allegedly infringing content when legally required or otherwise appropriate.
  • Notify the creator who published the content.
  • Restrict further distribution or monetization while resolving the complaint.
  • Record the complaint for repeat-infringer enforcement.

Ganime responds expeditiously to legally sufficient DMCA takedown notices.

Removing content does not necessarily mean Ganime has made a final determination that copyright infringement occurred.

Ganime may also remove or restrict content when we otherwise become aware of infringement or reasonably determine that removal is necessary under our policies or applicable law.

6. If Your Content Is Removed

If your content is removed following a copyright complaint, Ganime will take reasonable steps to notify you as required by applicable law.

The notice may include:

  • Identification of the affected content.
  • The reason access was disabled.
  • Information about the copyright complaint, where appropriate.
  • Instructions for submitting a counter-notification if you believe the removal resulted from mistake or misidentification.

You should not repeatedly re-upload the same material to bypass a copyright removal.

If you believe your content was removed by mistake, you may use the counter-notification process described below.

7. How to Submit a DMCA Counter-Notification

If Ganime removed or disabled your content in response to a DMCA takedown notice, and you believe the material was removed because of a mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notification.

Your counter-notification must include:

  1. Your signature. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the removed content. A description of the material and its location on Ganime before it was removed, including its URL if available.
  3. Good-faith statement. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your contact information. Your name, address, and telephone number.
  5. Jurisdiction and service statement. A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the Federal District Court for the judicial district where your address is located, or, if your address is outside the United States, any judicial district in which Ganime may be found, and that you will accept service of process from the person who submitted the original copyright notice or their agent.

Submit your counter-notification to Ganime's designated copyright agent listed in Section 11.

Important: A counter-notification is a legal statement. Submit one only when you genuinely believe your material was removed due to mistake or misidentification.

Ganime may forward your counter-notification, including the personal information required by law, to the original complainant.

8. Restoring Content

When Ganime receives a valid DMCA counter-notification, we will follow the process required by applicable law.

This generally includes:

  1. Promptly forwarding the counter-notification to the original complainant.
  2. Informing the complainant that the removed material may be restored.
  3. Restoring the material no earlier than 10 business days and no later than 14 business days after receiving the valid counter-notification, unless our designated agent first receives notice that the complainant has filed a qualifying court action seeking to prevent the allegedly infringing activity.

Restoration under this procedure does not establish that the creator owns the content or that its use is lawful.

Ganime may continue to restrict material for an independent violation of its policies or applicable law.

If a dispute involves complex ownership questions, the parties may need to resolve their rights through the appropriate legal process.

9. Repeat Copyright Infringers

Ganime maintains a policy of terminating the accounts of repeat copyright infringers in appropriate circumstances.

We may consider factors including:

  • The number and nature of copyright complaints.
  • Whether the complaints are sufficiently supported or resolved.
  • Repeated uploading of previously removed infringing material.
  • Evidence of deliberate infringement.
  • Valid counter-notifications, retractions, or other relevant information.
  • The seriousness and circumstances of each violation.

Enforcement measures may include:

  • Copyright warnings.
  • Content removal or restricted publishing.
  • Temporary suspension of monetization.
  • Restrictions on uploading new content.
  • Account suspension or permanent termination.

Ganime may terminate accounts for serious infringement without requiring multiple prior warnings.

We will reasonably implement this repeat-infringer policy and accommodate, without interfering with, applicable standard technical measures used by copyright owners to identify or protect copyrighted works.

10. Creator Earnings & Copyright Violations

Creators may earn revenue when viewers purchase access to eligible content on Ganime.

If content is removed or restricted because of suspected copyright infringement, Ganime may temporarily hold earnings related to that content when reasonably necessary to investigate claims, handle refunds or chargebacks, or comply with applicable law.

Where infringement is established, Ganime may:

  • Stop future monetization of the affected content.
  • Adjust earnings associated with refunded or reversed transactions.
  • Restrict withdrawals where legally permitted or required.
  • Take further action consistent with the Creator Agreement and applicable law.

Ganime will not automatically confiscate unrelated, legitimate Creator Earnings simply because a copyright complaint was received.

Payment adjustments and unresolved earnings will be handled under the Ganime Creator Agreement and applicable law.

Copyright reports and counter-notifications must be submitted in good faith.

You should consider whether the reported use is authorized by the copyright owner or by law, including potentially applicable copyright exceptions, before submitting a takedown notice.

Ganime may take appropriate action against users who abuse copyright-reporting mechanisms, including by knowingly submitting fraudulent complaints.
