Delete your Ganime account

Ganime, by Fresh Road Studio Inc.

You can delete your Ganime account and its data at any time, from the app, from the website, or by email. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

In the app or on the website

  1. Open the Ganime app, or go to ganime.io/account, and log in.
  2. Open the Account tab, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation and tap Delete permanently.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

By email

If you can no longer sign in, email ganimesupport@freshroad.com from the address registered to your account, with the subject “Delete my Ganime account”. We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and profile: email address, display name, profile picture, password and linked Google or Apple sign-in.
  • Your comments, likes and ratings, and the reports you filed.
  • Your watch history, favorites and block list.
  • Your Quick Create projects, and every series you created that nobody else has bought.
  • Your credit balance. Remaining credits are forfeited and cannot be refunded or transferred.

What is kept

  • Series other people have bought. They stay available to those buyers, with your name removed, and are hidden from browsing. This protects purchases other users have already paid for.
  • Backups. Copies in our database backups are overwritten or deleted within 90 days.
  • Records the law requires. Where a law requires us to keep a record, for example for tax or fraud prevention, we keep only that record, for as long as that law requires.
  • App store purchases. Apple and Google keep their own records of purchases made through them, under their own policies.

Delete some of your data and keep your account

You can remove parts of your data without deleting your account. In the Ganime app or at ganime.io/account, open the Account tab, then:

  • Watch history: Watch History → Clear History.
  • Favorites: Favorites → Clear Favorites, or remove a single series.
  • Profile picture: Overview → Remove Avatar. You can also change your display name there.
  • Your series: My Series → open a series → Delete Series. A series someone has already bought can't be deleted, because it stays available to its buyers.
  • Block list: Settings → Blocked users → Unblock.

Changes take effect straight away. For anything else, such as removing your comments, email ganimesupport@freshroad.com from your account's email address and tell us what to delete. We will do it within 30 days. Copies in backups are removed within 90 days, as described above.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy. Questions: ganimesupport@freshroad.com.
